## 1. Evaluation protocol
- **Data:** the canonical Task 7 split (assignment SHA-256 `47fb02a87b36…`). The TEST split has 758 memes: 382 non-misogynistic, 376 misogynistic, 21 empty OCR captions.
- **Task:** binary `misogyny_detection`, the only enabled task.
- **Scope, fixed before unlocking (developer-approved):**
  - the 3 final models;
  - the traditional baseline B07;
  - all already-trained ablation models: B01–B06, T01–T04, I01–I02, M01–M03.

  That is **19 models**, evaluated **once** each.
- **Decision rule:** argmax (LR probability > 0.5, SVM margin > 0, softmax argmax). No threshold was tuned.
- **Populations:**
  - primary: all 758 samples;
  - secondary: captions not empty (737); without the `doublemean*` watermark (744).
- **Statistics, frozen in advance:**
  - 95% percentile bootstrap: 10,000 resamples of the test samples, seed 42; paired for differences.
  - Exact McNemar test with Holm correction for the **two primary comparisons** (M04 vs T05, M04 vs I03).
  - "Significant" requires Holm p < 0.05 **and** a CI excluding 0. All other comparisons are descriptive.
- **Answer rules for the research questions** were frozen in the manifest. The verdicts in §16 are generated by code from those rules.

## 17. Discussion
**[INTERPRETATION]**
- **On MIMIC, the picture is the dominant signal.**
  - A frozen SigLIP image embedding with a strongly regularised logistic regression reaches 0.805 macro-F1 on unseen memes.
  - Every text model stays at 0.62–0.67, despite fine-tuning a Hindi/Hinglish-specific encoder.
- **Fusion as built does not exploit the caption beyond the image.**
  - M04's text branch reproduces T05 exactly, and its contribution on top of the image is nil on TEST.
  - The Task 11 validation gain over the *untuned* image model (+4.9 points) shrank after tuning the image baseline (Task 12) and vanished on TEST.
  - Image-only and multimodal are statistically equivalent.
- **The validation-to-test drop is model-specific.**
  - Validation to test, the TF-IDF models fell 2.2–3.3 points and T05 fell 3.9 points.
  - T04 and the frozen MuRIL models were stable (−0.3 to +1.8), and the image models held or rose (I03 +0.5, I01 +1.8).
  - [HYPOTHESIS] Text-side choices (normalization variant, TF-IDF settings, T05 over T04) were selected on noisier signals and partly overfit the validation set. The image regularisation choice generalised: I01 → I03 is +0.028 on TEST, with a CI excluding 0.
- **Normalization:** Hinglish spelling normalization, a core idea of the project, did not help any model in a measurable way, and it may hurt fine-tuned MuRIL.
  - [HYPOTHESIS] A pretrained multilingual tokenizer already handles common spelling variants. Normalization removes surface variation that carries some signal.
- **Practical choice for the application:**
  - I03 is as accurate as M04, needs no OCR, and is far cheaper.
  - However, the image model's suspected **gender-presence shortcut** (Task 10) and the image-led nature of M04 mean that both can flag harmless photos of women. The application should present results as **WARNING / HUMAN REVIEW**, not automatic decisions (project policy).

## 18. Limitations
- **One dataset, one split, one seed** for the evaluated artifacts. There is no external test set.
- **The captions are MIMIC's own OCR.** The application's OCR (Task 14) may behave differently.
- **Shortcuts** (gender presence, empty caption, page watermarks) are present in the data. Their contribution to TEST scores is not removed.
- **Scores are uncalibrated.** LR image scores are overconfident; MuRIL softmax scores are compressed.
- **The text representation inside fusion** was fine-tuned on the same training labels (T05). The frozen-text variant (M03) performed slightly worse on TEST (−0.010, CI includes 0).
- **The manual error review** covers only 24 of the 149 M04 errors.

## 19. Threats to validity
- **Internal:**
  - Validation reuse across Tasks 8–12 makes validation scores optimistic; the TEST evaluation addresses this.
  - Several ablation comparisons are run, but only two were pre-declared as primary and Holm-corrected; all others are descriptive.
  - Fusion training is bit-exact only for the same CPU thread count; evaluated artifacts are fixed by checksum.
- **Construct:**
  - The label "misogynistic" comes from MIMIC annotators. Some labels look debatable; they were flagged, not changed.
  - Macro-F1 treats both classes equally; the costs of FP and FN differ in moderation.
- **External:** results may not transfer to other meme sources, newer templates, other languages or real user uploads. Image-source artifacts (resolutions, pages) may differ.
- **Statistical:** the bootstrap assumes independent samples. Near-duplicate groups were kept within splits (Task 7), but within-test template reuse may still correlate errors.

## 20. Final conclusions
- **[FINAL TEST RESULT]** The best systems are **image-only I03 (0.8047)** and **multimodal M04 (0.8031)**. They are statistically indistinguishable.
- **Multimodal outperforms text-only** (RQ2 SUPPORTED, +0.156).
- **Multimodal does not outperform image-only** (RQ3 NOT SUPPORTED).
- **Hinglish normalization does not improve classification** (RQ1 INCONCLUSIVE, leaning negative).
- **No model was changed after TEST.** The selected finals (T05, I03, M04) remain the reported final models, even though T04 scores higher than T05 on TEST. Any model built from these insights would be a new, separately reported experiment.
- **Literature comparison:** `existing_system_comparison_template.csv` is prepared with no values filled in. Literature values must be verified in the paper task before any comparison is claimed.
